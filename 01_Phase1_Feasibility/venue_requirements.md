## One venue only (blueprint §1)
- MLESP notifies on Oct 18, which is after DL4BBDA's Oct 1 deadline. **Once the paper is submitted to MLESP, it cannot go to DL4BBDA this year.**
- The honest decision point is therefore **Sep 26**. If every Phase 1–8 exit condition is met, submit to MLESP. If not, don't submit; the work continues toward DL4BBDA (Oct 1), and after that EMBC 2027 (blueprint §16).

## What differs from the blueprint
- The blueprint's §9 word budget assumed 8 pages *excluding* references (about 6,500 words). MLESP is 8 pages *including* references, so the text budget is smaller. Per §9, "recompute once the page limit is confirmed" (see advisor questions).
